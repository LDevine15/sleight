# App-class speed map, 2026-10-19

The speed map times the ChatGPT engine on one owned fixture per app, with no model in the loop. A sequential trial times
five steps: acquisition, a first and a warm full read, one action and a readback. A cohort runs four read-only sessions
on one fixture concurrently. Every cell ran 10 trials with the owner away, on engine 26.1013.40934. The data, row by
row, is in [2026-10-19-speed-map.json](2026-10-19-speed-map.json), and the method is in
[benchmark.md](../benchmark.md#app-class-speed-map).

The results come from three runs, because Cursor and Excel needed harness fixes after the first:

## Sequential trials

Times in ms, median / p90 (nearest rank) over 10 trials. Setup runs from the return of `open` to an identified fixture
window. Nodes counts the numbered rows of the first full read.

| Class | App | Complete | Setup | Acquire | First read | Warm read | Action | Readback | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apple native | Finder | 10/10 | 12 / 19 | 1189 / 1923 | 103 / 126 | 109 / 131 | 1083 / 1144, scroll | 94 / 109 | 76 |
| Apple native | TextEdit | 10/10 | 6 / 12 | 1335 / 1517 | 194 / 346 | 147 / 272 | 1228 / 1273, scroll | 76 / 81 | 19 |
| Electron | VS Code | 10/10 | 1010 / 1159 | 1277 / 1663 | 221 / 273 | 194 / 269 | 1178 / 1207, scroll | 123 / 137 | 169 |
| Electron | Cursor | 10/10 | 1682 / 1698 | 895 / 931 | 114 / 150 | 82 / 92 | 1028 / 1040, scroll on the prompt box | 80 / 86 | 102 |
| Office | Word | 10/10 | 1099 / 1121 | 1250 / 1457 | 240 / 299 | 154 / 189 | 1367 / 1401, scroll | 127 / 141 | 120 |
| Office | Excel | 10/10 | 845 / 899 | 1356 / 1373 | 58 / 59 | 58 / 61 | 684 / 749, Page Down key | 70 / 80 | 169 |

Cursor's and Excel's action times measure something other than a document scroll.

- Cursor 3.25 opens an empty profile in its agent-first layout. Its 102-node tree has buttons, toolbars and one text entry
  area, most likely the agent prompt box, with no list, table or scroll area. The fixture folder's row never appears, so
  the scroll went to that box.
- Excel's tree doesn't list its cells. Through the engine, a scroll on the formula bar failed in 8 of 10 trials and on the
  workbook canvas in 10 of 10, so Excel's action is a Page Down key that Excel handles itself. It isn't comparable with
  the scroll rows.

## Concurrent cohorts

Four read-only sessions on one fixture, 10 cohorts per app. Times in ms, median / p90 over the sessions that completed.
Cohort wall time covers session start, both reads and cleanup, over the cohorts where all four sessions completed.

| App | Sessions complete | Acquire | First read | Warm read | Cohort wall time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TextEdit | 40/40 | 1335 / 1550 | 455 / 521 | 353 / 451 | 3446 / 3538 (n=10) |
| Excel | 29/40 | 4422 / 4515 | 184 / 241 | 196 / 249 | 6185 / 6404 (n=7) |

In Excel 11 of 40 acquisitions failed. Cohorts 4 and 10 lost all four sessions, and cohort 5 lost three. Every failure
returned the same engine error, without an AX code (signature 431effdb), and none answered busy, so the harness didn't
retry. Failures that take a whole cohort point at that cohort's Excel fixture or engine connection more than at four
sessions competing. We don't know the cause. Earlier that day, Excel's cohorts passed 40/40 at 09:31 UTC and 20/40 at
09:44 UTC.

## Focus and covers

The runs recorded what the fixture apps did to focus on their own, and none of it stopped a cell.

- VS Code and Cursor activated themselves at launch in 10 of 10 trials each (`launch-activation`), before any engine call.
- When cleanup quit Cursor, focus returned to the app that had it before in 9 of 10 trials (`focus-restored`).

## Not covered

Notes and Calendar are refused, because no qualified way exists to prove the fixture container is local. Helium needs a
browser-profile receipt, and Spotify must already be running, so neither ran. The owner-present apps (Slack, Discord,
WhatsApp, Notion and Mail) need the owner at the Mac.

Each app has one run of 10 trials, so we don't know how much these numbers move between runs or under load. The Mac's
load during the runs wasn't recorded.
