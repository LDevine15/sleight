## First-call queueing by session count

`bench/fowl/coldstart-probe.mjs` (no model) timed each step of a first call, with 1, 4 and 13 sessions started together, split
across Chrome and Helium, at 17:30 EDT.

| Sessions | Connect | Empty first call | `getBrowser` | Tab open, mean | Tab open, slowest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 185 ms | 190 ms | 54 ms | 914 ms | 914 ms |
| 4 | 259 ms | 296 ms | 98 ms | 1,744 ms | 1,902 ms |
| 13 | 577 ms | 1,263 ms | 152 ms | 3,151 ms | 4,231 ms |

FowlBrowser's runner starts 13 sessions at once, and sleight's first call took 4.8 s (median) in the 15:23 round. From 1 to 13
sessions the tab open grows by 2.2 s, in the Codex extension, which sets up every tab in one queue. Startup (connect and the
empty call) grows by 1.5 s, and that part is sleight's server and the engine's REPL starting together. A user running one session
waits about 1.3 s in all.
